In the field

Every observation starts the same way.

01 · Occupy

Set up over the point

Tripod centred on the control point, legs footed, plate roughly level. Nothing downstream is better than this step.

02 · Orient

Level, backsight, close

Level the tribrach, sight a known backsight and close the loop before a single new point is collected.

03 · Observe

Measure and check on site

Angles and distances tied to the network and checked in the field — not back at the office three days later.

Reality capture

Ten million points, resolved into something you can build from.

  1. Raw returns
  2. Registered cloud
  3. Surface mesh
  4. Deliverable
0 points

  1. The scanner sees everything

    A UAV or terrestrial scanner returns millions of unordered points — every surface in range, with no meaning attached to any of them.

  2. Registration ties them together

    Separate scans are aligned to shared control, so the cloud sits in your project’s coordinate system rather than its own.

  3. Surfaces are extracted

    Ground, structure and built elements are separated and meshed — the point of the exercise, and the part that takes judgement.

  4. You receive something usable

    Existing conditions in the format your team already works in, checked before it leaves us.
